# 🎧 Listening Set 01: Part 1 & Part 2

## 📍 Part 1: Car Rental Booking (Form Completion)

### 🎧 Audio Script Excerpt
> **Customer:** I'd like to rent a car for next week, starting on Monday, August 17th.  
> **Agent:** Great. And how many days will you be needing it?  
> **Customer:** Well, initially I thought about 5 days, but let's make it 7 days so I can return it on Sunday.

### 📝 Questions
* **Q1.** Length of rental period: `[ ______ ]` days
* **Q2.** Pick-up Date: August `[ ______ ]`

> #### 💡 만점토익 S-Class Tip
> * **Self-Correction 덫 방어:** "5 days"에 낚이지 말고 "make it 7 days"로 수정한 최종값(`7`) 선택.
> * **스펠링/대문자:** August 등 월 이름은 대문자 필수.

## 📍 Part 2: Community Center Floor Plan (Map Labeling)

### 🎧 Audio Script Excerpt
> **Guide:** As you enter through the main entrance, the reception desk is in front of you. If you turn right and walk straight down the corridor, past the cafeteria on your left, the **Seminar Room** is the last door on your right before the garden.

### 📝 Question
* **Q3.** Where is the Seminar Room located?
  - (A) Opposite the main entrance
  - (B) Next to the cafeteria on the left
  - (C) At the end of the right-hand corridor

> #### 💡 만점토익 S-Class Tip
> * `Turn right and walk straight down` $\rightarrow$ Right-hand corridor
> * `Last door` $\rightarrow$ At the end of the corridor (정답: **C**)
